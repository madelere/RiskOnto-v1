### Counting methodology — verified against the prior-established convention, not assumed

**The manuscript's "substantive RDF triples" convention was checked explicitly, not assumed
identical to raw `len(graph)`.** The only prior documented use of the word "substantive" in this
project's evidence trail (`DERIVED_FROM_FINDING_AND_RULE_COUNT_RESOLUTION.md`, line 52) draws a
specific line: *"the substantive 103,554-triple ABox is unchanged — the +2 is purely this metadata
addition"* — i.e., the established convention already excludes newly-added **TBox axioms and
property-level annotations** (schema/documentation statements) from "substantive," while counting
all **ABox facts about individuals** (including materialized/rule-derived assertions) as
substantive. `FINAL_ONTOLOGY_COUNT_REGENERATION.md` independently confirms the frozen original's
103,554 was itself obtained by plain `rdflib` parse — for that specific file, raw and substantive
coincide because it contains zero non-substantive (TBox-only) additions relative to itself.

Applying this same convention to the consolidated file, isolating exactly which triples changed at
each step (filtering out blank-node relabeling noise from SWRL rule-body `rdf:List` structures,
which is a cosmetic rdflib re-serialization artifact — confirmed by an unchanged blank-node-involving
triple count, 324, at every step):

| Step | Raw triples | Named-node-only diff vs. previous | Classification |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `RiskOnto_global_v27.owl` (frozen) | 103,554 | — (baseline) | substantive = raw = 103,554 |
| `RiskOnto_global_v27_riskfixed.owl` | 103,906 | +352 `hasRiskLevel` (new ABox assertions on `RiskAssessment` individuals) net of a 352/352 `criticalityValue` relabel swap (net 0) | **All +352 substantive (ABox)** — same category as the already-counted 19,671 rule-materialized assertions |

**Substantive count for the consolidated file: 103,554 + 352 (ABox) + 0 (TBox/annotation excluded)
